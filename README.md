# Sunkeeper — The Secret of Abu Simbel

A SPECS Lens for the "Learn AR with SPECS" hackathon: walk around a tabletop model of the Great Temple of Ramesses II (built c. 1264–1244 BCE), find the one spot where the dawn sun stands on the alignment days, and watch the light travel through the halls to the sanctuary. Design brief and build notes live in `devpost/`.
